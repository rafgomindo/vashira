## 2. Privacy Policy
Vashira is designed with **Privacy by Default**.
- **No Tracking**: Vashira does not include analytics, telemetry, or behavioral tracking.
- **Local Data**: Your sqlite database, papers, and notes stay on your machine. 
- **Shadow Backend**: Vashira connects to public APIs (Crossref/DOI) to fetch metadata. These external services have their own privacy policies.
- **Sync**: When you enable "Collaborative Sync" (Phase 3), your data is encrypted and synced only to nodes you authorize.
