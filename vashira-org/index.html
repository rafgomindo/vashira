The Meaning of Mastery

The name वशीर (Vaśīra) is derived from Sanskrit, meaning "The one who possesses mastery" or "Self-controlled." Vashira is built on the principle that your research is your own dominion. Unlike cloud-locked platforms, this program grants you total sovereignty over your intellectual capital.
