Privacy Policy

Applies to the Vashira desktop app (version 10.2 and later). Last updated: 22 November 2026.

In short: Vashira is local-first. Your library, your PDFs, your notes and your annotations stay on your computer. There are no accounts, no analytics, no telemetry and no ads. Vashira only connects to other computers for the specific features listed below. Most of those are off until you turn them on, and the rest only send what you look up.

What stays on your computer

Your library database, the files you import, your notes, annotations, tags, collections and settings are stored in Vashira's data folder on your device. None of it is uploaded anywhere by Vashira. API keys you enter (for an AI provider or Zotero) are stored encrypted with your operating system's keychain.

When Vashira uses the network

Sharing with Vashira users on your network off until you opt in

Vashira asks you once whether to share. If you say yes:

  • When you import a paper, its title and DOI (or a fingerprint of the file) are announced to other Vashira users on the same local network (for example, a university Wi-Fi).
  • Other Vashira users on the network can ask your copy of Vashira for an item's bibliographic details (title, authors, year, abstract, DOI/ISBN, journal, volume, issue, pages, publisher, edition and file fingerprint).
  • Files, notes, annotations, tags, collections, file locations and your personal categories are never shared.

To find each other, running copies of Vashira also broadcast a small presence signal on the local network containing a random identifier that changes every time the app starts. Like any network traffic, these messages reveal your device's local IP address to other devices on the same network. You can change the sharing choice at any time in Settings.

Global Mesh off unless you enable it

If you enable Global Mesh in Settings, Vashira registers with the community relay at peers.vashira.io so peers outside your local network can find you. The relay receives your random node identifier and, as part of any internet connection, your public IP address, and gives you the addresses of other participating peers. Vashira also asks your router (via UPnP) to open a port for peer connections, and closes it again when you turn Global Mesh off or quit.

Looking up bibliographic data when you use it

To fill in or verify metadata, Vashira sends the identifiers or search terms you use (DOIs, ISBNs, arXiv IDs, titles or keywords) to public scholarly databases: Crossref, arXiv, Open Library and OpenAlex. Citation styles not built into Vashira are downloaded from the public Citation Style Language (CSL) style repository on GitHub. These services receive your IP address and the request, as with any website.

The Discover tab and Related papers send your search terms, or the DOI or title of the item you're looking at, to OpenAlex. If you add an optional OpenAlex API key in Settings, it is sent with those requests. Add with PDF downloads a free, legal open-access copy directly from the publisher or repository that OpenAlex lists for it (never from other Vashira users); that site receives your IP address like any download.

Zotero import only if you configure it

If you enter a Zotero user ID and API key, Vashira uses them to read your Zotero library from Zotero's servers and copy item metadata into Vashira. Nothing is written back to Zotero.

AI assistant (the Oracle) only if you configure it

If you configure an AI provider, your question and the most relevant excerpts from your library are sent to the provider you chose (for example a local server on your own computer such as Ollama, or a cloud service with your own API key). That provider's own privacy policy applies. Vashira does not send anything to an AI provider you haven't configured.

Web page snapshots when you capture a page

When you save a web page with the Vashira browser extension, Vashira opens that page itself, in the background, to save a PDF snapshot of it.

Browser extension and Office add-ins

The Vashira browser extension and Word/Office add-ins send what you capture only to the Vashira app on your own computer (localhost). Vashira's local endpoints accept connections from your computer only, not from other devices on the network.

What we don't do

  • No user accounts, no tracking, no analytics or telemetry, no advertising.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data. The Vashira project doesn't operate any server that receives your library.
  • No peer-to-peer file sharing. Peers only ever exchange bibliographic metadata.

Downloads and updates

If you install Vashira from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft handles the download, installation and updates under Microsoft's privacy statement. Downloads from GitHub are covered by GitHub's privacy statement.

Your control

  • Change network sharing and Global Mesh at any time in Settings.
  • Delete individual items (Trash → Empty Trash removes them and their stored files).
  • Uninstalling Vashira and deleting its data folder removes everything it stored.

Because Vashira keeps your data on your device and doesn't send it to the Vashira project, there's no copy of your library held by us to access, correct or delete. Requests about data held by third-party services (Crossref, OpenAlex, Zotero, your AI provider, Microsoft, GitHub) should go to those services.

Changes and contact

If a future version changes what Vashira sends over the network, this page will be updated before that version is released. Questions: open an issue at github.com/rafgomindo/vashira.
